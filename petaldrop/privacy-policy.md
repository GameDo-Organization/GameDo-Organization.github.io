# Privacy Policy for Petal Drop

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

GameDo Studio ("we", "our", "us") built Petal Drop as a free, ad-supported game. It has no in-app purchases. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

Petal Drop does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. We do not require account creation, login, or registration. All game progress is stored **locally on your device**. The only exception goes to Google: optional Google Play Games features (see below).

### Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Petal Drop uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Advertising identifiers (Advertising ID on Android)
- Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
- IP address (used for approximate location, not stored precisely)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can review your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy" button in the game's settings.

### Google Play Games Services (optional)

On Android, Petal Drop uses **Google Play Games Services** for achievements (the game's trophies) and leaderboards (best run, daily challenge). Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts; you can also decline or sign out. When you are signed in, Google receives and processes:

- Your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar)
- The scores and achievements you send from the game, which other players may see according to your Play Games profile visibility settings

GameDo Studio does not receive your Google account details. This data is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Games Services terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms).

## How Ads Work in Petal Drop

- **Rewarded ads:** you may choose to watch a short video to receive a reward (a second chance, new charms, doubled petals, a second daily gift). This is always optional.
- **Interstitial ads:** never during a run and never in your first minutes of play; at most one after every three finished runs, and none if you just watched a rewarded ad.

## Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using Godot Engine's built-in file storage. This includes:

- Petals, records (best score, best shot, best multiplier), wins and seasons unlocked
- Charms, seeds, themes, trails and flowers unlocked, herbarium and trophies
- Daily challenge, daily gift, quests and day streak
- A run in progress, so it can resume where you left it
- Sound, music, vibration and language settings

This data is never transmitted off your device, except the scores and achievements sent to Google Play Games when you are signed in. Uninstalling the app will delete all saved data.

## Children's Privacy

Petal Drop is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Since we do not collect any personal data ourselves, no special provisions are needed. However, Google AdMob may serve ads according to its own policies regarding children's content.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been exposed to inappropriate content through ads, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Choices

- **Ad tracking:** You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).
- **Ad consent:** In the EEA and the UK, use the "Ad privacy" button in the game's settings to change your ad consent.
- **Google Play Games:** You can sign out, change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings on your device or at [play.google.com/games](https://play.google.com/games).
- **Delete data:** You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
