# Privacy Policy for Dip'Dee Survival

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

GameDo Studio ("we", "our", "us") built Dip'Dee Survival as a free, ad-supported game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

Dip'Dee Survival does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require account creation, login, or registration. All game progress (level, gems, upgrades, unlocked skills, nights survived, settings) is stored **locally on your device**. The only exception is optional: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your achievements and your best Endless Night time are sent to Google (see below).

### Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Dip'Dee Survival uses **Google AdMob** to display rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
- Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
- IP address (used for approximate location, not stored precisely)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. 

### Google Play Games Services (optional)

On Android, Dip'Dee Survival uses **Google Play Games Services** for achievements and the Endless Night leaderboard (your best survival time). Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts; you can also decline or sign out. When you are signed in, Google receives and processes:

- Your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar)
- The scores and achievements you send from the game, which other players may see according to your Play Games profile visibility settings

GameDo Studio does not receive your Google account details. This data is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Games Services terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms).

## How Ads Work in Dip'Dee Survival

Dip'Dee Survival uses **rewarded video ads only**. Ads are never shown automatically or forced on the user. In the in-game shop, you may choose to watch a short video ad to receive bonus gems. This is entirely optional and does not affect core gameplay.

## Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using Godot Engine's built-in file storage. This includes:

- Player level and experience
- Gems balance and purchased damage upgrades
- Unlocked boss skills
- Last night reached and best night (high score)
- Whether the intro has been watched
- Audio volume, mute, language and auto-fire settings

This data is never transmitted off your device, except your achievements and best Endless Night time when you are signed in to Google Play Games. Uninstalling the app will delete all saved data.

## Children's Privacy

Dip'Dee Survival does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we do not collect any personal data ourselves, no special provisions are needed. However, Google AdMob may serve ads according to its own policies regarding children's content.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been exposed to inappropriate content through ads, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Choices

- **Ad tracking:** You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:
  - **iOS:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  - **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **Google Play Games:** You can sign out, change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings on your device or at [play.google.com/games](https://play.google.com/games).
- **Delete data:** You can erase your game progress by tapping "Reset progress" in the Options screen of the title menu, or delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
