# Privacy Policy for Quadrille

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

GameDo Studio ("we", "our", "us") built Quadrille as a free, ad-supported puzzle game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

Quadrille does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require account creation, login, or registration. All game progress is stored **locally on your device**. The only exception is optional: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your best Classique score is sent to Google to appear on the leaderboard (see below).

### Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Quadrille uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
- Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
- IP address (used for approximate location, not stored precisely)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can review your choice at any time with the "Privacy" button in the game's settings.

### Google Play Games Services (optional)

On Android, Quadrille uses **Google Play Games Services** for the Classique mode leaderboard (your best score). Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts; you can also decline or sign out. When you are signed in, Google receives and processes:

- Your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar)
- The scores and achievements you send from the game, which other players may see according to your Play Games profile visibility settings

GameDo Studio does not receive your Google account details. This data is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Games Services terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms).

## How Ads Work in Quadrille

- **Rewarded ads:** you may choose to watch a short video to receive a reward (an extra hand, a bonus, a gift, sunshine). This is always optional.
- **Interstitial ads:** from level 10 onward, an ad may be shown between two levels, at most once every few minutes. Ads are never shown after a lost level.

## Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using Godot Engine's built-in file storage. This includes:

- Stars earned on each level and the classic mode best score
- Bonuses, harvest, garden layout and herbarium progress
- Winning streak, daily gift and sunshine (energy) status
- Sound, music and language settings

This data is never transmitted off your device, except your best Classique score when you are signed in to Google Play Games. Uninstalling the app will delete all saved data.

## Children's Privacy

Quadrille does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we do not collect any personal data ourselves, no special provisions are needed. However, Google AdMob may serve ads according to its own policies regarding children's content.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been exposed to inappropriate content through ads, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Choices

- **Ad tracking:** You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:
  - **iOS:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  - **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **Ad consent:** In the EEA and the UK, use the "Privacy" button in the game's settings to change your ad consent.
- **Google Play Games:** You can sign out, change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings on your device or at [play.google.com/games](https://play.google.com/games).
- **Delete data:** You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
