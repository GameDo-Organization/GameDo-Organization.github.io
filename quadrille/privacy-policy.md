# Privacy Policy for Quadrille

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

GameDo Studio ("we", "our", "us") built Quadrille as a free, ad-supported puzzle game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

Quadrille does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require account creation, login, or registration. All game progress is stored **locally on your device only** and is never sent to any server.

### Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Quadrille uses (or will use, in versions that include ads) **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
- Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
- IP address (used for approximate location, not stored precisely)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Test versions of Quadrille may show placeholder ads that are drawn by the game itself. These placeholders do not contact any server and collect no data.

## How Ads Work in Quadrille

- **Rewarded ads:** you may choose to watch a short video to receive a reward (an extra hand, a bonus, a gift, sunshine). This is always optional.
- **Interstitial ads:** from level 10 onward, an ad may be shown between two levels, at most once every few minutes. Ads are never shown after a lost level.

## Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using Godot Engine's built-in file storage. This includes:

- Stars earned on each level and the classic mode best score
- Bonuses, harvest, garden layout and herbarium progress
- Winning streak, daily gift and sunshine (energy) status
- Sound, music and language settings

This data is never transmitted off your device. Uninstalling the app will delete all saved data.

## Children's Privacy

Quadrille does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we do not collect any personal data ourselves, no special provisions are needed. However, Google AdMob may serve ads according to its own policies regarding children's content.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been exposed to inappropriate content through ads, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Choices

- **Ad tracking:** You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:
  - **iOS:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  - **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **Delete data:** You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
